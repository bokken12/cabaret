Safeguards: checks that refuse an action unless the caller allows them. land checks unreviewed and non-owner, rebase checks non-owner, each inside its transaction after the action's hard errors, returning the refused safeguards (NEVec) rather than an Error; Store::transact_or_abort aborts without writing. Each action states its safeguards in its types (LandSafeguard/LandAllow, RebaseSafeguard/RebaseAllow), widening into Safeguard for generic frontends. land_safeguards/rebase_safeguards preview them. CLI: per-action --allow-unreviewed / --allow-non-owner, with refusals naming the flag. napi: Landed/Rebased unions plus the preview queries; VSCode reports refusals as a stopped step until safeguards-vscode asks.
